02 / The person behind it
One foot in science.
One in technology.

I’m Uchechukwu Divine Chukwujeme, an Environmental Management and Toxicology student with a growing interest in technology, digital tools, and analytical problem-solving.

My perspective connects environmental systems with the tools we use to understand and improve them — from field observations and technical reporting to learning how websites and data workflows are built.

This portfolio is a living record of what I’m learning, what I’ve worked on, and what I’m building next. The aim is simple: make useful work, explain it clearly, and keep getting better.
